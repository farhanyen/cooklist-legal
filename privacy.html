Privacy Policy

CookList is a weekly meal planner. We designed it to collect as little as possible.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences (dietary needs, mood, appliances, budget, cook days, store), your meal plan and your grocery list are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data, and there is no CookList account.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the Sunday reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device.

Tracking

CookList does not track you across apps or websites and shows no ads.

Children

CookList is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
