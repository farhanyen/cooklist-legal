CookList Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

CookList is a weekly meal planner for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

Your answers during setup (household size, dietary needs, allergies, foods you won't eat, budget, cook days, store and appliances), your meal plans and your grocery list are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated by the app and your purchase history from Apple. It does not receive your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Apple's handling of payment information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Recipe images

Recipe photos are loaded from Unsplash. Like any website, Unsplash receives your device's IP address when an image is downloaded. See Unsplash's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (such as a weekly planning reminder or a reminder before your free trial ends) are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

What we don't do

CookList has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites. We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

CookList is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
